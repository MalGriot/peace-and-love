Bio

Born and raised between Brooklyn and Queens, New York, on soul records and church harmonies, Mal Griot is a vocalist, spoken-word artist, MC/host, and voice actor now based in India. On camera and behind the mic alike, his work is built on presence, rhythm, and control: every performance treated as an experience rather than a routine.

Recent on-camera work includes lead commercial spots for Zoho CRM and Zoho Cliq. Behind the mic, his range spans character and animation voice acting, audiobook narration (25 distinct characters voiced for Forget Me Not by JT Ezra), guided meditation VO, and commercial VO.
