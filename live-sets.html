Three ways to book Mal Griot

One artist, three distinct offerings, chosen by what the room is for, not sold off a fixed menu.

01. Flexible flagship show

Mal Griot | Live

Originals, covers, live looping, voice, rhythm and improvisation, shaped to the room and drawn out with the audience, with djembe where the setting calls for it. The adaptable core of the practice: the same identity, scaled to fit.

30 MIN60 MIN90 MINEXTENDED

Extended bookings accommodate longer event engagements and multiple sets across an evening, up to roughly four hours: a night's residency, not one continuous concert.

02. A complete performance work

Breathe Love Deep | Live

The live expression of breathe love d e e p: ten pieces about stillness, breath, and the space between one thought and the next; about love, loss, longing, and learning to let go. Built on groove and restraint, not range. This isn't a flexible set. It's treated as an experience rather than a concert, where transitions, lighting, atmosphere and silence carry as much weight as the notes.

Full sequence, repertoire and staging concept available on request via the EPK.
