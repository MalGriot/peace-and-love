Who It's For

How the Program Works

Built for the swimmers who plateau alone.

Most self-taught swimmers hit a wall around the same point: they can move through water, but efficiency and breathing hold them back. 12 sessions of structured, cohort-based coaching closes that gap directly.

What to bring: Swimsuit, towel, goggles, water bottle Equipment provided: Pull buoys, fins [confirm]
FAQ

Confident Swimmer-specific questions.

If you can swim a length unaided but tire quickly or lack technique, this is the right level. If unsure, book an Assessment session first.

It builds the endurance and control foundations open water requires, though open-water-specific coaching is not the primary focus of this program.
