Who It's For

Complete beginners, and anyone uneasy in water.

If you've never had formal swimming instruction, or water still feels unpredictable to you, Foundation is the correct starting point, regardless of age. This program assumes no prior ability.

You'll Learn
  1. Water confidence: entering and exiting safely
  2. Breath control
  3. Putting the face underwater
  4. Floating: front and back
  5. Body awareness in water
  6. Streamline and basic kicking
  7. Basic freestyle
  8. Basic backstroke and independent swimming

How the Program Works

A cohort, not a drop-in class.

You join a fixed group of up to 6 students who progress together across 8 sessions over 4 weeks. This keeps the pace consistent and lets Akshay track real progress against the curriculum, instead of repeating fundamentals every session for whoever shows up.

What to bring: Swimsuit, towel, goggles, water bottle Equipment provided: Kickboards, teaching aids [confirm]
