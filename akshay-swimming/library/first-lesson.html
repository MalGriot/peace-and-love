What to Expect From Your First Swimming Lesson

What actually happens in a first session, and why it matters.

Most first-lesson anxiety comes from not knowing what's about to happen. Here's exactly what a first session looks like, so there are no surprises.

Before you get in

Akshay starts with a short conversation: your comfort level, any past experiences with water (good or bad), and what you want out of the program. This takes a few minutes and shapes how the session is paced.

In the water

The first session never assumes ability. Even in group cohorts, the opening session focuses on comfort: safe entry and exit, orientation in the water, and establishing a baseline for where each student is starting from.

What you won't be asked to do

You won't be asked to swim a length, put your face underwater before you're ready, or keep up with anyone else in the group. The first session is about establishing trust, in the water and in the coaching.

By the end of session one

Most students leave the first session with a clear sense of what they'll be working on over the following weeks and, just as importantly, with less anxiety than they walked in with.

What to bring to your first session: swimsuit, towel, goggles, water bottle, flip-flops and a change of clothes. Full equipment list on the FAQ page.
