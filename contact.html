General

A Queens, New York-born vocalist, spoken-word artist, MC/host, and voice actor, now based in India. The work spans Afro-house, funk, and soul, with singing, writing, hosting, and voice acting treated as one continuous instrument rather than separate careers. More of the story on the About page.

A griot is a West African storyteller, musician, and oral historian — used here to describe the role itself: singer, poet, host, and voice, all serving the same instinct to tell a story and move a room. More on that on the About page.

A catalog that spans Afro-house, funk, soul, hip-hop, neo-soul, jazz, R&B, spoken word, ambient, and cinematic — including "I Tried It" with producer Unnayanaa on Wind Horse Records, which earned airplay on BBC Radio 6 Music. More in Releases.
