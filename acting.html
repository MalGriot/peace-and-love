Why Mal

Character built from rhythm, not just lines.

That practice carries straight into character work: musicality in the way a line lands, physical presence built from years on stage, improvisation when the take needs to bend, and a storyteller's instinct for what an audience is actually feeling. Presence isn't performed — it's rehearsed, every night, in a different room.
