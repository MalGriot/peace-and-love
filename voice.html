Origins

American, born and raised in Queens, New York — now based in India. The sound didn't change address so much as add one, carrying that low end and close-mic delivery into Afro-house, funk, and soul.

Singer, songwriter, poet, live looper, MC/host, voice actor — treated as one continuous instrument rather than separate careers. The through-line is the voice: sung, spoken, looped, or handed to a character, whichever the moment calls for.

The catalog moves freely across Afro-house, funk, soul, hip-hop, neo-soul, jazz, R&B, spoken word, ambient, and cinematic — a refusal to sit still in one genre long enough to be filed under it. Curiosity and freedom over a fixed lane.

Work Ethic & Accolades

"I Tried It," with Bangalore producer Unnayanaa on Wind Horse Records, earned airplay on BBC Radio 6 Music and coverage from Magnetic Magazine and MIXMAG — a fusion, as one review put it, of "house grooves and poetic storytelling."

The catalog keeps building: "Helicopter Man" with the band Deep Dawn, the solo productions "Overmind" and "Sun Burna," and the full-length breathe love d e e p — ten tracks about stillness, breath, and the space between one thought and the next.

None of it ships on the first draft. Every lyric, poem, and bio gets cut and reworked, sometimes dozens of passes, in pursuit of emotional truth over commercial trends — authenticity and craftsmanship over easy polish.

Personality

Every performance is built to feel like an experience, not a concert. Transitions, lighting, atmosphere, and silence matter as much as the notes — the pause before a verse does as much work as the verse itself.

Off stage, the instinct is the same one that shapes the songs: a systems thinker who builds his own frameworks, currently sorting ninety-plus poems by theme rather than chronology, chasing the shape of the thing before the thing is finished.

No patience for managed, softened feedback — wants it straight, the same way he wants the work itself. "Create work that people return to years later."
